Shipping Timelines

  • Accra: 1–3 business days
  • Rest of Ghana: 3–5 business days
  • International: 7–14 business days, varies by destination and customs processing

Shipping costs and exact timelines are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and are shown before payment.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS. If tracking hasn't updated within 48 hours, contact us and we'll follow up with the courier directly.

Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached. To start a return, use the link in your order confirmation email or reach out via the Contact page with your order number.

  • Returns are free on domestic orders
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the return
  • Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return
  • Accessories (caps, bags, belts) must be returned with any original packaging

Exchanges

Need a different size or colour? Select "Exchange" instead of "Refund" when starting your return. Exchanges are subject to stock availability at the time your return is received.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with a photo and your order number — we'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.
