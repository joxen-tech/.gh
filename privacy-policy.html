Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Placeholder policy for prototype purposes

This page is a template. It illustrates the structure a real privacy policy would follow but is not a substitute for one reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction before launch.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse or shop with dryrobe®, we may collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, shipping address and phone number at checkout — along with information collected automatically, like pages viewed, items added to your cart or wishlist, and general device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To communicate with you about your order, account or enquiries
  • To improve the site's products, layout and performance
  • To send marketing communications, only if you've opted in

3. Local Storage & Cookies

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart, wishlist and recently viewed items between visits. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to a server in this prototype build. A production build would also use cookies for analytics and session management, disclosed here with opt-out options.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers strictly to fulfil orders (such as payment processors and delivery couriers), or where required by law.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
